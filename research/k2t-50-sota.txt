K2,t-minor-free minimum dominating set: factor-50 SOTA audit
Checked 2026-11-16

Reported issue
The source row pic-large-50-dom was already indexed, but had no reviewed SOTA
annotation. It was therefore hidden by SOTA only. This was a curation omission,
not a certified dominance comparison. Bare K2t was also missing as a search
alias. The record correctly belongs to Parameter dependent, not Constant.

Primary sources
1. Bonamy, Gavoille, Picavet and Wesolek (PODC 2025):
   https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01091v2
   https://dept-info.labri.fr/~gavoille/article/BGPW25.pdf
   Theorem 4.1 gives factor 50 in O_t(1) rounds, displayed f(t), for t >= 2.
   The approximation factor is independent of t. Do not read the subscript t
   as a multiplicative O(t) runtime. Theorem 4.4 separately gives factor 2t-1
   in exactly three rounds. Neither point is removed by an uncertified
   comparison between its full round dependence and that of the other.

2. Amiri and Siebertz (October 2026), Table 2 and Section 8.4:
   https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09618v2#S8.SS4
   Explicitly retains the approximation envelope min{t+2+epsilon, 2t-1, 50}.

3. Amiri, Siebertz and Wawrzyniak (November 2026), Theorem C(i):
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.02057
   The factor 2a+1 against the fractional optimum, with minor density
   a=(t+1)/2, gives t+2 in parameter-dependent broadcast CONGEST rounds.
   The related-work discussion still records the factor-50 result.

4. Chudnovsky, Reed and Seymour, The edge-density for K2,t minors,
   Theorem 1.1 (author manuscript, PDF page 3):
   https://web.math.princeton.edu/~mchudnov/K2t.pdf
   For every integer t >= 2, every simple n-vertex K2,t-minor-free graph
   satisfies |E| <= (t+1)(n-1)/2. The class is minor closed, so every minor
   has density at most (t+1)/2. Substituting this rational value into ASW26
   Theorem C(i) gives t+2 for every t >= 2; there is no threshold at 8.
   ASW26 Corollary 1.1 and Appendix B restrict the different coefficient
   2t-3 on K_t-minor-free graphs to 3 <= t <= 9. These graph classes and
   parameter ranges must not be conflated.

Annotation decision
Add pic-large-50-dom as a best-known uniform-in-t approximation tradeoff,
with the conservative visible scope "Ratio independent of t; t >= 49".
For t <= 25 the three-round factor 2t-1 is smaller. The newer generic bound
has factor below 50 for t <= 47; at t=48 it ties 50 with a stronger fractional
benchmark and broadcast CONGEST model. These observations motivate the
annotation's restricted domain, not a global dominance edge: the unspecified
round functions have not been certified comparable on every parameter.
No matching lower bound, tightness, or optimal parameter dependence is claimed.
The original theorem and its t >= 2 applicability remain unchanged.

Discovery and display
Add bare K2t, K2,t and TeX K_{2,t} aliases. Canonicalize the display label to
K₂,t-minor-free; preserve the source spelling in graphClassSource. No graph
inclusion or problem reduction is added by this change.
