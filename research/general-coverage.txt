General and recent-results coverage

25 papers, 62 bounds. Primary sources inspected on 15 November 2026. The second pass added 7 papers and 27 bounds, focused on 2024–2026 arXiv and the cuttable-problems citation trail.

Scope: selected foundational constant-in-n, log-star, degree-dependent, logarithmic, polylogarithmic and sublinear algorithms. Includes anonymous vertex/set cover, bounded-degree matching, CONGEST weighted matching, independent sets, positive covering/packing LPs, local covering, local-density estimation, sleeping-CON­GEST domination, and recent sparse-graph domination. This is a curated starting catalog, not an exhaustive literature history or a claim that every row is currently best known.

Recent additions include arXiv:2409.16168 (Li–Ouyang–Wang fractional packing/covering), 2411.12694 (Christiansen–van der Hoog–Rotenberg density), 2506.10845 (Faour–Kuhn independent sets), 2511.08210 (Izumi–Kitamura–Yamaguchi matching), 2605.13264 (Davies-Peck matching/vertex cover), 2608.19096 (Ji–Pai–Pemmaraju sleeping domination), and 2611.02057 (Amiri–Siebertz–Wawrzyniak short-broadcast covering). Earlier records already include 2610.09618, 2610.15596, 2610.31203, and cuttable meta-theorems 2605.19157. All preprints are labeled in the bibliography.

Verification means a primary source states the recorded result, not independent proof checking. Some entries are abstract-level; theorem locators are supplied only when checked. Each record carries a direct primary-source URL. New results have no inferred best-known or dominance label.

Source caveats: Li–Ouyang–Wang (2024), Remark 1.2 (https://arxiv.org/html/2409.16168v1), reports a flaw in Ahmadi–Kuhn–Oshman (2018)'s weighted fractional matching analysis. Three AKO18 rows are marked status=claimed and carry visible caveat/caveatUrl fields. Two FFK21 matching rows inherit a caveat because FFK21 Lemma 5.2 explicitly uses that weighted primitive. FFK21 vertex cover is not marked disputed: Appendix A.3 reduces to unweighted fractional matching, so the weighted proof issue cannot simply be propagated to it. No independent repair was checked; do not present these five claims as unqualified achieved bounds or automatically choose them as best known.

Comparability: the Amiri–Siebertz minor-closed result uses the class minor-density parameter a(C) and a fractional optimum benchmark. Picavet's shallow-minor result uses maximum average degree d plus bounded depth-one shallow-minor density. These have different hypotheses and are retained separately. The November Amiri–Siebertz–Wawrzyniak preprint states exact constant-round broadcast-CONGEST factors 7 for planar and 5 for triangle-free planar against fractional OPT, but its factor 7 on arbitrary fixed Euler genus is against integral OPT. Set-cover factor 4+ε in that paper has bounded positive integer costs and planar incidence, with no bound on set sizes/frequencies; it is not an exact factor 4. Covering-LP approximations are fractional outputs; local-cover approximation schemes are integral outputs.

Parameter discipline: fixed degree, weight range, genus, sparsity or 1/ε is stated whenever necessary. A log-star label means log-star for the explicitly fixed parameters, not for growing Δ or 1/ε. Faour–Kuhn's tight tree/path bound assumes ε > log(n)/n. Maximization fractions use achieved-value/OPT unless a stronger total-input-weight benchmark w(V) is explicitly displayed. Izumi et al.'s bound retains the maximal-matching subroutine MM(N), rather than silently hiding polylog(n) and classifying it constant. Sleeping CONGEST lists both total rounds and awake rounds. Local-density records estimate a per-vertex value rather than claiming to output a densest subgraph. Logs in asymptotic expressions are truncated at constants where customary.

Other limits: the FFK21 vertex-cover lower bound was checked in Theorem 1.5 and applies to randomized as well as deterministic algorithms. BCGS17 fast randomized matching rows require consulting the full paper for instance-dependent success-probability formulations. Broadcast CONGEST and sleeping CONGEST are represented by model=CONGEST with the restriction/resource spelled out in variant/assumptions. The cuttable k-tuple definition only requires vertices outside the chosen set to have k chosen neighbors.

Explicit omissions: the complete corrected Kuhn–Moscibroda–Wattenhofer lower-bound family; comprehensive randomized LOCAL matching developments; every packing/covering tradeoff; and systematic pre-2024 set-cover history. Boppana–Halldórsson (SIROCCO 2025, DOI 10.1007/978-3-031-91736-3_9) is a relevant follow-up: its publisher abstract describes a one-round independent-set result with high probability and a small loss, but the precise loss and parameter conditions were not recovered, so no numerical row was fabricated. The search also surfaced fault-tolerant spanner papers (including arXiv:2410.07844); their stretch/absolute-size guarantees were not conflated with approximation of minimum spanner size. No recent cut-optimization row was added without a primary source giving the matching LOCAL/CONGEST round/ratio theorem. These are expansion opportunities, not claims of nonexistence.
