CITATION TRAIL AND NEW COVERAGE
Locally-nice / cuttable bibliography -> Czygrinow et al., TCS 2019 broadcast-CONGEST domination:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304397518304894
Publisher abstract and indexed Lemma 10 specify the model and ten-round implementation. Exact ratio was not reconstructed from the publisher's broken mathematical rendering; the row says O_g(1). Genus is orientable, not arbitrary Euler genus. The later 91+epsilon conversion uses additional LOCAL processing and is not relabeled as a short-message result.

Cited domination authors -> Marcin Witkowski's research list -> ICTCS 2017 / TCS 2020 k-domination:
https://mw.web.amu.edu.pl/research.html
https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-1949/ICTCSpaper05.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2019.12.027
The accessible conference version supplies exact locators and years. The extended journal version is recorded in paper metadata but not treated as independently checked. Convention: only vertices outside the chosen set require k neighbors in it. This is not closed-neighborhood k-tuple domination.

Same author trail -> distance domination in K2,t-minor-free graphs:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.03229
Added Theorems 15, 18, 19. Preserved the connectedness/diameter assumptions of the first two theorem statements. Did not silently insert an extra small-diameter brute-force reduction. Theorem 19's inequality is gamma_1 <= C gamma_k; introductory prose has the inequality in the unhelpful reverse direction.

Distance-domination related work -> Amiri and Wiederhake 2019 preprint / 2022 journal:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.02794
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304397522000081
https://publikationen.sulb.uni-saarland.de/bitstream/20.500.11880/34562/1/1-s2.0-S0304397522000081-main.pdf
The original preprint supplies precise upper/lower records. The journal abstract expands to four covering problems and uses girth 4r+2 rather than the preprint's 4r+3. The institutional journal PDF failed to open, so journal records are expressly abstract-level, leave the factor unspecified beyond constant, and use Unspecified randomization. These are version-specific records, not an assertion that the preprint is the final journal text. Algorithm-specific worst cases were not misclassified as general lower bounds.
