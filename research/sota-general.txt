Meaning and limits

Ten annotations concern mathematically tight bounds or approximation-scheme families with the optimal limiting ratio. Three concern newer best-known general-graph matching/vertex-cover tradeoffs, supported by explicit comparisons in primary papers and targeted searches for later developments. A preprint's theorem remains a source claim; this audit does not independently certify its proof. The underlying bibliography visibly labels preprints.

“Optimal limiting coefficient” is deliberate: planar-incidence set cover has 4+ε for every fixed ε>0 and a lower bound below 4. No exact factor-4 algorithm is asserted. Likewise the covering schemes approach ratio 1; their dependence on ε/δ is not claimed optimal. Tight log-star statements concern n with the stated fixed parameters, except the tree/path independent-set theorem which also proves the displayed 1/ε dependence in its allowed range.

The broader matching/vertex-cover flags must preserve the detailed scope. Davies-Peck's matching probability is not the usual 1−n⁻c guarantee. Its n-only upper bounds improve the large-degree worst case and do not dominate degree-dependent algorithms at small Δ. Izumi–Kitamura–Yamaguchi is maximum cardinality, not maximum weight; MM(N) remains a maximal-matching subroutine. This is a fully polynomial 1/ε, polylog(n) tradeoff, not a claim of pointwise dominance over all degree-dependent algorithms or over gathering an entire graph when ε is extremely small.
