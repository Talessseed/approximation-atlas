42 bibliography records represent all 42 sections of Feuilloley, arXiv:2001.08510v4. 53 result rows from 32 of those sections were checked against primary-paper abstracts, theorem statements, or the authors’ conference abstract. This is not an exhaustive theorem extraction, nor a complete or current literature review.

Conventions: LOCAL is a conservative model assignment when stronger bounded-message support was not checked. Anonymous rows describe ID-free algorithms and preserve message assumptions in text. Maximal independent set subroutines are not misclassified as maximum-independent-set approximations. The k-tuple papers use outside-D domination, which differs from closed-neighborhood k-tuple domination. Fractional results explicitly say so. Publication year follows the cited version; preprint years are used when recorded, but early versions are not exhaustively resolved.

Primary checks that refine the informal survey:
- 2018 K_h-minor domination paper also gives constant approximation for excluded topological K_h and a (1+ε) scheme for excluded K_h minors.
- Distance domination on bounded expansion works in broadcast CONGEST, with O(r² log n) rounds.
- Dory–Ghaffari–Ilchi weighted domination is CONGEST and assumes polynomially bounded positive integer weights.
- Alipour–Futuhi–Karimi’s theorem uses m=0 and random tie-breaking; no isolated vertices. It gives 32 for MDS and 16 for total domination.
- Alipour–Jafari conference abstract states planar graphs without 4-cycles (not necessarily triangle-free), and randomization.
- Anonymous planar domination differs by version: journal guarantee is 694; older references quote 636.
- Minimum domination schemes use a factor at least 1; survey notation 1−1/polylog n cannot be reused literally for minimization.
